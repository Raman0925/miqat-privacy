Miqat Privacy Policy

Applies to the Miqat Android app (package com.miqat.app). Developer: Raman Kumar Kannaujiya. Last updated: 6 November 2026.

Summary

Miqat does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. Everything the app does happens on your phone.

  • No accounts. No sign-up, login or user ID.
  • No servers. We do not operate a backend, so there is nowhere for your data to go.
  • On-device calculation. Prayer times, the Hijri calendar, Qibla, alarms and widgets are computed on your device.
  • Approximate location only. If you allow it, the app uses coarse location (about 1 km), only while the app is in use, and rounds it to 2 decimal places before saving it on your phone. It never requests precise or background location. You can type a city instead and grant no location permission at all.
  • No ads, analytics or trackers. No advertising, analytics or attribution SDKs, and no access to the advertising ID.

Network requests

The app only connects to the internet when you open a Quran surah that is not already cached:

ServicePurposeWhat is sent
quranenc.comQuran text and translationsThe surah and translation requested. No identifiers, no location.
cdn.islamic.networkQuran recitation audioThe audio file requested. No identifiers, no location.

These requests use HTTPS. Like any web request, they reveal your IP address to that service, which is governed by its own privacy policy. Everything else works offline.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, location label and rounded coordinates, prayer tracker, tasbih counts, cached Quran text and a local alarm reliability log are stored only on your device. You can erase them in Settings → Privacy → Erase all data, or by uninstalling the app. If you have enabled Android's device backup, your settings may be included in that backup, which is encrypted by Google and not accessible to us.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. All app data is under your control on your device. This applies under the GDPR / UK GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
